# Tadween for Claude

Plan, draft, schedule and publish social media posts from Claude, in English and Arabic. The plugin connects Claude to the
Tadween MCP server at `https://studio.tadween.app/mcp-oauth-claude`; you sign in to your own Tadween account (OAuth), no API key.

## What you need
- A Tadween account with at least one connected social account (https://tadween.app, 14-day free trial). The connector is part of the Team and Agency plans.

## What it can do
List your connected channels, check a channel's posting rules, create drafts or scheduled posts, attach media from a link or
from your device, and review or update upcoming posts. Claude asks for confirmation before write actions. It never deletes posts.

## Privacy Policy
https://tadween.app/privacy covers what Tadween collects (account data, connected-channel tokens, your posts and media), why,
who it is shared with (the platforms you connect and the providers that run Tadween), how long it is kept, and how to delete it.
Contact: support@xerk.io.
